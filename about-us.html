About FM

Every stitch
has a story.

From a small factory in Taizhou to a nationwide network across Bangladesh — here's how FM became the name behind the machines.

1995

Jack is founded

Jack Sewing Machine Co., Ltd. began as a small factory in Taizhou, Zhejiang Province, China, focused on reliable, cost-effective sewing machines. Over the next decade it expanded its product line and refined its manufacturing, laying the groundwork for what would become a global brand.

Early 2000s

From factory to global brand

Jack diversified into specialized machines for different textile segments and expanded into Southeast Asia, Africa and Latin America. By the 2010s, Jack was known worldwide for quality, innovation and after-sales support — the same standard FM was built to bring to Bangladesh.

2004

FM opens its doors

FM, short for Fast & Modern, began as FM Business Associates, founded by M Fazle Karim as the first distributor to bring Jack Sewing Machines into Bangladesh. The office opened in Dhaka before relocating to Chittagong, where the market grew from the ground up.

2011

One company, two names

As the business expanded nationwide, including back into Dhaka, the company was restructured and renamed Jack Machinery Import & Export. Today the group also operates as Impressive Technology & Service, so either name may appear on an invoice or service paperwork — both are FM, backed by the same team, warranty and after-sales support.

Today

Trusted across the industry

FM works alongside some of the region's most established names, including Regency, Pacific Jeans, Kenpark, Shangu, Intex Link, Asian Group, Premier and Azim Group, alongside Groz-Beckert as needle partner. Those relationships are what keep machines running and factories moving.

Culture

The team behind the machines

FM's culture is built on collaboration and a straightforward commitment: keep every machine on the floor running. That means training technicians properly, stocking the parts factories actually need, and answering the phone when a line goes down.
