Anti Bird Nest Technology

No tangles.
Ever.

Bird's nesting, the chaotic thread tangle that forms beneath the fabric when sewing starts, is one of the most common causes of machine downtime and fabric waste. The A5E-BNX eliminates it entirely at the source.

  • Thread clamp holds the needle thread firmly at stitch start, preventing loops from forming beneath the fabric
  • Zero rework, with no stopping to clear tangles between garment pieces
  • Works consistently across light, medium, and heavy fabrics without adjustment

Ultra-Short Thread Trimming

Shorter ends.
Steadier every time.

Every thread tail left on a garment means extra seconds of manual trimming per piece, and across thousands of pieces per day, that is hours of lost productivity. A self-developed dual-stage linkage thread cutting system gives the A5E-BNX the shortest, most stable thread ends in its class.

  • Front thread ends ≤4mm, rear thread ends ≤3mm, on every single stitch, not just on average
  • Eliminates the manual trimming step after sewing, freeing operators for higher-value tasks
  • Consistent cut length regardless of fabric thickness or thread type

New From Groz-Beckert

Keeps its cool
at full speed.

At high sewing speeds, or on dense, multi-layer materials, friction can heat a standard needle enough to melt thread and scorch the stitch hole. The Litespeed™ needle's patented blade geometry shrinks the contact area with the fabric, cutting friction, and the heat, at the source.

  • Reduced friction and heat from a smaller contact area with the material
  • Gentle treatment of thread and material
  • Fewer thread breaks when the machine stops
  • Less downtime, higher process reliability
Litespeed™ · GB 100
